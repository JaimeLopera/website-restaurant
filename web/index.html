An omakase counter on the Nagara river, where everything is made by hand.

Chef Sōta Kanemoto grew up among the forges of Seki and spent years cooking his way across Japan before coming home to the river. Each evening he serves a kaiseki menu shaped by the season, from the Nagara, the Hida mountains and the Sea of Japan, on knives forged by his father and plates fired by his mother.

Our story

物語
一

Seki · childhood

The forge

Sōta grew up among the sparks of his father's forge. Before he ever held a kitchen knife, he learned that a blade is finished only when nothing more can be improved.

二

Fifteen years

The journey

At eighteen he left with a knife roll and little else. He cooked his way across Japan, from ryokan kitchens in the Hida mountains to the fish markets of the Sea of Japan.

三

Oze, on the Nagara

The return

He came home to open a single counter on the bank of the river: ten seats of pale hinoki, his father's knives and his mother's plates.

四

Early summer

The name

On summer nights, fireflies rise from the grass outside the window. In Japanese they are called hotaru: a small light by the river, which is all this restaurant wants to be.
